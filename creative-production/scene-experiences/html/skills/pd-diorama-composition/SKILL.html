Diorama Composition

Trigger and objective

Stage foreground, subject and environment as a coherent place.

Inputs

Subject dimensions, environment concept and viewport range.

Workflow

Establish contact, perspective, grounded shadows and a motivated light. Separate foreground/midground/background by meaning. Design mobile framing rather than crop the desktop composition.

Read docs/CHOREOGRAPHY-AND-PRIMITIVES.md from the canonical pack root. In a PhenoDesign checkout that root is creative-production/scene-experiences/; in the standalone ZIP it is the extracted V3 root. Read only the related docs/recipes, not every skill.

Deliver

Layer/world layout, safe content zones, camera envelope and inspected silhouettes.

Critical alternative / failure case

Do not mistake a floating object plus gradient for a complete environment.

Non-negotiable boundaries

Keep accepted design tokens, editable source and source rights. Work on owned files/sessions and within approved tool/spend/network scope. Capture actual outputs, report blocked backends honestly, and use the existing journey/evidence authority rather than an agent-authored success flag. A cheaper or less animated alternative wins when it preserves the same meaning and task with better results.
