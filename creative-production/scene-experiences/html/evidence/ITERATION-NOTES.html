Visual review and iteration

The actual desktop arrival, construction and threshold frames, plus mobile layouts, were inspected. The subject is a staged optical instrument, not a product photograph.

Two fixes were made after inspection: a controlled dark veil protects text as the bright aperture crosses the composition, and the header/control spacing has a narrow 320 px layout rather than overflowing. The final browser checks ran after these fixes. The aperture changes the opening, beam and floor projection together; finish selection persists through macro/portal transitions. These are the specimen's meaningful agency.

Remaining art work is explicit: richer environment modeling, genuinely spatial material response, physical surface detail, more deliberate subject silhouette and manufactured components, transition polish, and native-source-to-real-renderer qualification. The SVG mechanism is a reviewable blocking medium, not a substitute for final scene art. The eight-second authored clip compresses the scene study; it is not recommended pacing for a final product narrative and cannot demonstrate live interaction.

The strongest next experiment is not another effect. Build this same small narrative with a qualified Blender source and a live/hybrid browser consumer, then compare it against the current SVG rendition for spatial readability, response and resource cost.
