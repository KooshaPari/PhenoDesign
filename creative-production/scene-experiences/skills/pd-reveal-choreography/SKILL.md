# Reveal Choreography

## Trigger and objective
Compose cutaways, assemblies, masking and macro reveals.

## Inputs
Subject relationships, scene timing and factual limits.

## Workflow
Choose one revealing change: separate along an assembly axis, section a shell, match-cut a detail or enter a portal. Name the invariant and consequence. Define hold/reverse/loading/failure states before adding easing.

Read `docs/CHOREOGRAPHY-AND-PRIMITIVES.md` from the canonical pack root. In a PhenoDesign checkout that
root is `creative-production/scene-experiences/`; in the standalone ZIP it is
the extracted V3 root. Read only the related docs/recipes, not every skill.

## Deliver
Reveal board, state mapping, boundary screenshots and simpler alternative.

## Critical alternative / failure case
A reveal must expose meaning, not merely conceal a slow asset load.

## Non-negotiable boundaries
Keep accepted design tokens, editable source and source rights. Work on owned
files/sessions and within approved tool/spend/network scope. Capture actual
outputs, report blocked backends honestly, and use the existing journey/evidence
authority rather than an agent-authored success flag. A cheaper or less animated
alternative wins when it preserves the same meaning and task with better results.
