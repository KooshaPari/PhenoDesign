# Authored scene-study film

`scene-study.mp4` is an eight-second, 960 × 554, 12 fps H.264 presentation derivative.
Its 96 source frames were captured from the same SVG scene evaluator used by the
interactive specimen, with explicit deterministic seek. The complete source-frame
sequence is retained under `evidence/browser/film-frames/`.

This is **authored presentation**, not a recording of a person completing a journey.
FFmpeg encoded the frames and the result was fully decoded and metadata-checked.
It is **not** a Remotion execution result. The Remotion adapter still requires its
actual installed dependency/renderer/consumer qualification. No audio is included.

The interactive specimen remains the place to exercise aperture, finish, section,
scroll, keyboard, hold, reduced motion and reset behavior. A movie cannot prove
those interactions. See `../evidence/VALIDATION.md`.
